# Admit confirmed read-only siblings under RunAuthority

Permit an explicitly confirmed, bounded group of independent read-only delegates
in a trusted predefined plan. A shared pure predicate governs both projected
legal actions and kernel admission, counting recorded intents before dispatch;
current WorkspaceAuthority identity is fenced again at admission and invocation.
Runner concurrency alone cannot grant this authority, and caller-shaped dynamic
plans cannot opt in by copying a policy or definition label.

The first group contains at most two local review lenses, followed by the
existing critic over every selected accepted lens. Uncertain or unrelated effects
continue to serialize admission. Cancellation and recovery retain each exact
worker's evidence and retirement obligations; cancelling a run does not free an
unsettled worker's owner-runner slot. Omitted policy retains historical serial
identities. This preserves ADR-0008's sole lifecycle authority while allowing
bounded parallel observation without incremental authority caches or a second
scheduler. Owner runner capacity remains an explicit startup limit rather than
an authority-wide limit on manually issued commands.
