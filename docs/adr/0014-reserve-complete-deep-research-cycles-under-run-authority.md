# Reserve complete deep research cycles under RunAuthority

Deep supplied-source research uses an additive trusted definition with two fixed
read-only angles and a fresh gap critic. RunAuthority derives a complete next
cycle from an exact registered read-only cycle-decision receipt, rather than
accepting model-shaped plans or adding a frontend controller. The existing
revision transaction appends all required angle, critic, decision and checkpoint
cards atomically. Accepted evidence stays immutable; repeated angle directives
coalesce and insufficient whole-cycle capacity admits none of that cycle.

A safe critic-proposed replacement question waits at an explicit same-run scope
checkpoint. Approval appends the effective question and reserves both angles plus
a fresh critic within the original elapsed budget; it never rewrites the Source
packet or fixed angles. Decline can finish only the exact valid original-scope
report. Scope-preserving cycles covered by the initial confirmation may advance
automatically. Copied labels, stale receipts and unobserved time confer no
continuation authority.

The deep diagnostic join alone may consume positively settled operational angle
failure as typed evidence. That failure remains a sticky completion block, and
whole-run cancellation admits no new critic. Each accepted native output retains
the legacy output-safety contract. Complete host-materialized terminal sets use
the existing larger research-input policy only behind the trusted deep role,
schema and validator binding. Quick research, review, historical receipt
identities and ordinary checkpoint-decline semantics remain unchanged.
